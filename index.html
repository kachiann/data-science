House Price Predictor — Rhineland-Palatinate

Why it matters

In regulated settings, a model has to explain its predictions, not just score well. Pairing an accurate model with SHAP explanations lets users see why a prediction was made.

Problem

Estimating fair property prices in a specific German region needs a model trained on local listings — national models miss regional pricing dynamics.

Approach

Built a local SQLite pipeline from 3,800+ regional listings, ran full EDA and feature engineering, and trained an XGBoost regressor with MLflow experiment tracking for full run lineage.

Outcome

R² of 0.75 on held-out data. Shipped with SHAP explainability and an LLM chat assistant, so users see why a price was predicted, not just the number.

3,800+ listings · R² 0.75 · SHAP explainability + LLM chat assistant

Maternal Health AI Assistant

Why it matters

Health information has to be right. Grounding every answer in a published guideline, instead of model memory, is what makes an AI assistant trustworthy enough to use in a health setting.

Problem

New mothers need fast, trustworthy answers on infant nutrition and breastfeeding — but medical guidance is scattered, and generic chatbots hallucinate on health questions.

Approach

Built an agentic RAG system: a LangChain agent decides whether to answer from a FAISS-indexed WHO/AAP/CDC guideline store, or call the USDA FoodData Central API live for nutrient data — keeping every response traceable to a source.

Outcome

Deployed live on Streamlit. Every answer traces back to a real guideline or live nutrition data — not model guesswork.

Clustering & PCA for Equity Portfolio Structure

Why it matters

Before adding model complexity, it pays to understand the structure of the data. Clustering and PCA show which holdings truly diversify and which carry the same underlying risk.

Problem

A strategies-desk research project needed to understand how a 50-stock large-cap portfolio actually co-moves — which holdings are genuinely diversifying and which are redundant bets on the same underlying risk.

Approach

Applied hierarchical clustering with a correlation-based distance metric, selecting the linkage method via cophenetic correlation (average linkage, 0.875), and ran PCA on standardized daily returns to decompose variance into interpretable risk factors.

Outcome

Uncovered 5 economically coherent clusters (a distinct biotech cluster, a defensive Walmart outlier) and showed a single market factor explains 41% of return variance, with ~20 components needed for 80% — a clear diagnostic for concentrated vs. genuine diversification.

50-stock universe · 20 years of daily returns · 5 clusters · PC1 = 41% of variance

Deep Learning for Multi-Asset Return Forecasting

Why it matters

A model that fits training data but fails out of sample is a liability. Testing against simpler baselines and held-out data shows whether added complexity actually helps.

Problem

A research team needed to know whether deep learning architectures (MLP, CNN-GAF, LSTM) could reliably forecast 25-day-ahead returns across five asset classes — equities, long and short Treasuries, gold, and oil — and whether pooling assets into one joint model helps or hurts.

Approach

Built the data pipeline and EDA (ADF stationarity tests, correlation structure, 15 years of daily returns for SPY/TLT/SHY/GLD/DBO), designed a 3-layer stacked LSTM with multi-horizon lagged-return features (1/5/10/21/63-day) to predict 25-day-ahead returns, and authored the single-output vs. multi-output architecture comparison.

Outcome

The LSTM achieved positive in-sample R² on 4 of 5 assets, but that edge mostly didn't survive out-of-sample testing, and the multi-output model underperformed single-output LSTMs across all five assets — concrete evidence that added architectural complexity introduced noise rather than the intended cross-asset regularization.

5 asset classes · 15 years of data (2008–2022) · 3 architectures benchmarked (MLP, CNN-GAF, LSTM)

NourishMama — Cloud-Native ELT & Data Warehouse

Approach

Outcome

A live Streamlit dashboard non-technical users filter by baby age and audience, translating raw data into direct food recommendations.
